## Overview

This vault is the shared knowledge base for the project's AI agents. It follows the
Noosphere protocol — see `NOOSPHERE.md` in the project root for the full specification.

Agents read from and write to this vault. Humans can open it in Obsidian to browse,
edit, and contribute knowledge. Changes are tracked via git.

## How it works

- **Agents** capture insights from their work (PRs, issues, conversations) as notes
- **Humans** add context, ideas, and corrections directly in Obsidian
- **Git** tracks all changes as backup and history
- **Spheres** emerge organically as notes grow into areas of thought
